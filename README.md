# Johan Fredin Knutzén

Programme Director and researcher in suicide prevention in transport systems and public environments, affiliated with the National Centre for Suicide Research and Prevention (NASP), Region Stockholm Health Care Services and Karolinska Institutet.

PhD candidate at Karolinska Institutet, with expected defence in 2026.

My work focuses on suicide prevention through structural and organisational interventions in transport systems and other public environments. This includes applied research, evaluation of preventive measures, implementation-oriented work, and collaboration with national authorities, transport organisations and international research networks.

I have professional experience as a clinical psychologist in child and adolescent psychiatry and from the Swedish transport system, where I have worked operationally as a train driver and traffic controller in the Stockholm metro, alongside consultancy work in railway safety, risk management and organisational development.

Research interests include means restriction approaches in transport systems, such as railway fencing, AI-based surveillance, and infrastructure-based preventive interventions.
